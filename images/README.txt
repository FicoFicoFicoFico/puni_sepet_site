IMAGES

The website currently uses online Unsplash slideshow photos, with these local SVG backgrounds as offline fallbacks.

To use your own photos:
1. Copy images into this folder, for example slide-1.jpg, slide-2.jpg, slide-3.jpg.
2. Open ../style.css.
3. Replace the background-image URLs in .slide:nth-child(1), (2), and (3) with url("images/slide-1.jpg"), etc.

For a logo image, add logo.png here and replace the YOUR LOGO text in index.html with <img src="images/logo.png" alt="Your brand" />.
